Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 13, 2026

Welcome to OverReact ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy while providing an engaging real-time cricket experience.

1. Information We Collect

To provide our services, we collect the following types of information via Google Firebase:

  • Account Information: Email address, display name, and profile picture provided through Google Sign-In or Phone Authentication.
  • User-Generated Content: Memes, captions, and comments you upload to the platform.
  • Interaction Data: Your votes on polls and "likes" on memes.
  • Device Information: Technical data provided by Firebase Crashlytics to help us fix bugs and improve performance.

2. Third-Party Services

We use industry-standard third-party services to power OverReact:

  • Google AdMob: To display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers and location data to serve personalized ads.
  • Firebase (Google LLC): We use Firestore (Database), Cloud Storage (Files), and Crashlytics (Stability).

3. Data Deletion & Rights

Compliance with Google Play Policies: We provide full control over your data. In the app settings, you can choose to "Delete Account." This will trigger a permanent Cascading Deletion, which removes:

  • Your user profile and authentication record.
  • All memes you have ever uploaded.
  • All associated images stored in our cloud storage.

4. Advertising

Our app uses Google AdMob to show ads. These ads may be personalized based on your interests. You can manage your ad preferences through your Android device's "Google Settings > Ads" menu.

5. Children's Privacy

OverReact is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be notified through the app.
